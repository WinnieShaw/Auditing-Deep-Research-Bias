# Estimator definitions

All targets are finite candidate-pool means. Opened Mean averages observed outcomes. Outcome Regression (OR) averages document-level predictions over the full pool. Sequential IPW and DR use the logged chosen-document and continuation probabilities. Self-normalized estimators divide by the realized sum of inverse-probability weights; they are stabilized finite-sample estimators, not identical unbiased estimators. Weight-clipped DR caps each inverse-probability factor at 5, 10, or 20. Full CESS clips raw DR to [-1,1] and then shrinks it toward OR. The no-stopping ablation removes continuation-probability information only. Simple shrinkage uses clip(o + lambda(a-o), -1, 1). Ranking sensitivity first averages replicates within task/rank/context, takes the absolute opposing-rank difference, averages contexts within task, then averages source tasks.

Controlled propensity perturbations are evaluated only in the simulator because PERSPECTRUM real logs omit the full candidate probability vector. Temperature rescales logged policy logits, Gaussian perturbations add deterministic seeded logit noise, estimated propensity uses only candidate features visible before the current selection, and poor overlap sharpens the policy distribution.
